# The Singularity Layer

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-06-10 network; 2026-09-24 SystemOne.

**Product / availability:** Compute network and specialized inference endpoint.

June 10 posts announce a live cloud network. A September 24 thread introduces SystemOne/Laya and publishes an endpoint at grid.x402compute.cc/v1/systemone using the convaiinnovations/laya model. Later posts describe local MLX and typed-decision models.

The supplied July 6 AI Machines launch date was not established by the captured primary posts. There is no verified comparable open-weight text-generation speed or gpt-oss price. A specialized decision endpoint and deploy-your-own compute product should not be silently classified as a fast hosted text-model API.

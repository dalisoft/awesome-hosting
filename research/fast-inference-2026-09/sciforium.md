# Sciforium

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-25.

**Product / availability:** Live hosted optimized GPU inference endpoints.

**Published provider URL:** <https://sciforium.com>.

Sciforium reports MiniMax M3 decode speed increasing from 82.0 to 289.5 output tokens/s per user on four AMD MI355X GPUs. The thread explicitly links live on-demand endpoints. It also discussed a roughly threefold Kimi K3 improvement on September 17.

This is an internal comparison. A reply reports a drafter bug initially reduced accuracy, then a fix reached 95% of the stock score on its math benchmark. Another reply acknowledges different prompt lengths for the optimized and stock tests. Do not describe the speedup as demonstrated identical-quality performance. No reference-model token rate, standard signup credit or permanent free tier was verified.

**Repository decision:** Added to Tokens with a published provider/API link. Numeric reference-model pricing and unknown free/trial fields are not invented.

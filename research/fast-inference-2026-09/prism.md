# Prism

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-24.

**Product / availability:** Live hosted LLM inference cloud.

**Published provider URL:** <https://prisminference.com>.

The founder introduced Prism’s LLM cloud on September 24 with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 547 output tokens/s. Its launch thread describes agent-tuned deployments, lower prices for requests with flexible scheduling windows, serverless LoRA and dedicated deployments.

This is a new LLM-cloud introduction, not proof of company formation: the same founder had announced a Prism video API in April. A September price promotion is temporary and is not used as a permanent rate. Credit requests receive DM invitations, which do not establish automatic self-serve signup credits. The founder says OpenRouter integration is not yet available. No gpt-oss rate or recurring free tier was verified.

**Repository decision:** Added to Tokens with a published provider/API link. Numeric reference-model pricing and unknown free/trial fields are not invented.

# RunAnywhere / Wally

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-21.

**Product / availability:** Live hosted API and BYOC/on-prem stack.

**Published provider URL:** <https://runanywhere.ai>.

The Wally launch lists GLM-5.3 Flash at 380 tokens/s, GLM-5.3 Max at 790, Qwen3.8-27B at 485 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 615. A thread reply claims TTFT below 300 ms. The stack supports its own hosted GPUs and customer infrastructure. The profile identifies RunAnywhere as YC W26. Signup is advertised with $5 credit and no card requirement.

The comparisons with Nebius, Fireworks and Z.ai are Wally’s own demonstrations. Launch replies report key-creation problems; the provider says service recovered after a traffic surge. Another reply says rates are shown in the console, without publishing a gpt-oss input/output pair. The $5 signup allowance is separate from a recurring free plan, which was not verified.

**Repository decision:** Added to Tokens with a published provider/API link. Numeric reference-model pricing and unknown free/trial fields are not invented.

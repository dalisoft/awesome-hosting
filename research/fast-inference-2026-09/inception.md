# Inception

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-08.

**Product / availability:** Live proprietary diffusion-model inference API.

**Published provider URL:** <https://inceptionlabs.ai>.

Inception introduced Mercury 2.5 on September 8, claiming more than 1,100 tokens/s on standard NVIDIA GPUs. The announcement offers access through its API, OpenRouter and Baseten. This adds a diffusion-model provider to the shortlist, rather than another open-weight autoregressive model deployment.

The model and decoding architecture differ from DeepSeek, GLM and gpt-oss; the raw rate does not establish a quality-matched winner. Preview discounts expiring September 7 are excluded. No gpt-oss offering, comparable reference price, standard signup credit or recurring free tier was verified in the reviewed X posts.

**Repository decision:** Added to Tokens with a published provider/API link. Numeric reference-model pricing and unknown free/trial fields are not invented.

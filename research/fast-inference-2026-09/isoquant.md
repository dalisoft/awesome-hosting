It reports GLM-5.3 Flash at $0.07/$0.20 per million input/output tokens, cached input at $0.014 and median TTFT of 452 ms. It advertises OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible interfaces, image inputs, streaming, tools, structured outputs and automatic caching. Its profile identifies YC F26.

452 ms is first-token latency, not generation throughput. The post does not give a tokens/s result. GLM rates cannot populate the README’s gpt-oss reference-price column. Dedicated endpoints are offered through contact, but the launch also describes self-serve API keys. No current gpt-oss rate or recurring free/signup allowance was verified.

# Underdog AI / Husky

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-21 engine; 2026-09-23 app update.

**Product / availability:** Local Apple Silicon inference engine and Mac app; invite access.

**Published provider URL:** <https://underdog.ai>.

A founder post advertises the Woof model running locally at up to 730 tokens/s on a MacBook, with a claimed advantage over MLX. A September 23 provider post describes enabling Husky in app version 1.2.3. Its current profile links underdog.ai, describes on-device operation and asks users to obtain an invite from a friend.

This establishes a local product rather than a hosted API. The complete MacBook configuration and controlled quality comparison remain unspecified. A separate provider post describes its model as 4B parameters; this is not comparable to much larger cloud models solely by token speed. No permanent plan price or standard free/signup allowance was verified.

**Repository decision:** Added from the user-supplied shortlist under Local inference. Availability and unknown pricing/allowances are shown explicitly.

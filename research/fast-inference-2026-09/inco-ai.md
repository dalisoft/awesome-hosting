# Inco AI

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-03 beta; 2026-09-17 model addition.

**Product / availability:** Live hosted GPU inference API.

**Published provider URL:** <https://inco.ai>.

Inco announced public beta on September 3 for Kimi K3, MiniMax M3 and GLM models. It reported GLM-5.3 Flash at 592.6 output tokens/s on September 9, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 532 on September 17. API access is linked at platform.inco.ai.

The provider’s Splash announcement is a separate local Apple Silicon engine: Qwen3.8-27B at 144 tokens/s on M5 Max. Do not combine this result with hosted-model rates. Claimed leaderboard positions are dated provider statements rather than independently checked current rankings. No gpt-oss price or standard free/signup allowance was verified.

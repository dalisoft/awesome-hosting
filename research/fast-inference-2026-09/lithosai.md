# LithosAI

**Review date:** 2026-09-26. **Observed announcement:** 2026-09-09 inferred; 2026-09-24 speed update.

**Product / availability:** Live hosted GPU inference API.

**Published provider URL:** <https://lithosai.com>.

A September 10 post says its public API went live the previous day, implying September 9. The September 24 update claims DeepSeek V4.1 Flash exceeds 650 output tokens/s per user. It also advertises Kimi K3 at 305 tokens/s in its fastest offering and a separate lower-cost tier at $1.85 per million blended tokens. The design offers different latency/cost service levels on shared GPUs.

These are provider claims. The blended Kimi rate is not an input/output pair and is not necessarily the fastest tier’s price. A developer reply questions speculative-decoding acceptance on real coding inputs. No gpt-oss price, standard signup allowance, or permanent free tier was verified; the supplied FRONTIER promotion is not recorded as a trial.

**Repository decision:** Added to Tokens with a published provider/API link. Numeric reference-model pricing and unknown free/trial fields are not invented.
